What is Leadership?

A perspective on leadership, initiative, and the importance of equal opportunity in technology.

"If your actions inspire others to dream more, learn more, do more and become more, you are a leader." I would define leadership as the ability to initiate, to process and work coherently on innovative ideas.

This experience taught me that not even a worldwide pandemic can stop growth if we desire to build our own way out of the fire. Leadership is about initiative, resilience, and the ability to bring people together around a shared goal.

A significant challenge is ensuring that everyone has equal opportunity and equal value for their work. Leadership grows when we uplift others and create space for diverse voices.
