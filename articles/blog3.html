Creative Writing: The Conflict

A short narrative about listening, empathy, and the difficult stories we carry.

It was a breezy morning when I visited the Singh's abode. Lavanya was helping her mother to serve breakfast. Her face had lost colour since we last met. I had never experienced such a quiet and grave Lavanya, although I've always wanted her to grow up an act wise like adults but I could not have imagined that my expectation would become unbearable for me.

She sighed, 'Look Akhil, I-a-a- I don't know what to say, and how to say. I can't figure out how to deal with what I am feeling right now.' The story became a conversation about guilt, regret, and the hard work of opening up.

'Please Lavanya, try to let your heart out. It will make you feel better.' The dialogue is a reminder that sometimes the best help is simply showing up and asking the right question.
